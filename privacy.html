Privacy policy

Updated 25 October 2026

Landslide collects no personal data. There are no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking, and nothing about you or how you play leaves your device.

Who I am

Landslide is made by Macauley Treleaven, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: macauleytreleaven@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved game and the name you give your Prime Minister.
  • Your settings: difficulty and haptics.
  • The careers, records, Prime Ministers and Leaders of the Opposition from your games, for the hall on the menu.
  • The latest copy of the polling and seat data, and this week's start.

These are kept only on your device. Delete the saved game in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything.

The one thing the game downloads

When it opens, the game downloads a public data file of polls and seat holders from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). The request carries no account, identifier or information about your game. As with any website, GitHub's servers see your device's IP address to send the file back; GitHub's own privacy statement covers that. I don't receive it.

Sharing

If you share a picture of the simulation, a verdict, a career or a week's result, or a game's code, iOS sends it wherever you choose. The game doesn't see where it goes. A code holds only how that game starts (its party, difficulty, starting polls and a number for its luck), nothing about you.

Buying the game and crash reports

You buy Landslide from Apple, who handle payment. I receive only Apple's summary sales figures, never who bought it. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage figures; you can turn that off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Landslide is suitable for everyone and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

These pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or scripts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security.

Your rights and changes

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have a concern, email me, or contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. If the game ever changes what it collects, this policy will be updated first, with a new date.
